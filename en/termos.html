2. What BipView is — and what it is not

BipView is a mirror: it brings together, in one place, what you hold in exchanges and wallets, and shows it in Brazilian reais and US dollars.

BipView:

  • does not buy, sell, or trade anything for you;
  • does not hold your coins — we are not a custodian and have no access to your funds;
  • does not provide virtual asset services on anyone's behalf: it does not carry out exchange, transfer, custody, or administration of other people's assets — and BIPVIA does not change that;

We are a visualization tool. Decisions about your money remain yours.

4. What you connect to BipView

You choose what to connect, and there are two ways — both read-only:

  • Exchange API key, read-only. Create the key without withdrawal and without trading permissions. We check and warn you on screen if the key can withdraw. The key is stored encrypted.
  • Public wallet address (or the extended public key, xpub). It is what you share to receive funds — it is already visible on the blockchain to anyone.

You can also upload a statement from an exchange to complete your history.

Never ask, never paste: BipView never asks for your seed phrase or private key, on any screen or through any channel. If anyone asks for it on our behalf, it is a scam.

By connecting a source, you declare that it is yours or that you are authorized to access it.

5. The numbers you see are estimates

The amounts shown come from public sources and from the exchanges themselves, and are converted using market prices. They are for tracking, not for checking to the last cent.

The price of an old transaction is our estimate, calculated for the minute the transaction happened. When we have no price for an asset or a date, the screen says so — we do not make up a number instead.

The official statement is your exchange's, and the truth of a transaction is what is on the blockchain. A discrepancy? The source rules, not us.

6. Taxes: a mirror, not a tax return

BipView calculates a tax estimate to help you understand your situation. It does not replace your accountant, is not tax advice, and is not your tax return.

The official tax return can be filed with Declare Cripto, our partner — and BipView receives a referral commission. We say so here because you have the right to know.

You are responsible for what is declared to the Receita Federal.
