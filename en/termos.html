2. What BipView is — and what it is not

BipView is a mirror: it brings together, in one place, what you hold on exchanges and wallets, and shows it in reais and in dollars.

BipView does not:

  • does not buy, sell or swap anything for you;
  • does not hold your coins — we are not a custodian, and we have no access to your funds;
  • does not give investment advice. Nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy or sell, nor securities analysis;
  • does not issue official documents for the tax authority or any other body.

We are a viewing tool. Decisions about your money remain yours.

3. Your account

To use BipView you need an account, created with a valid e-mail address. You are responsible for keeping your password secret and for everything that happens in your account.

The account is personal. Do not share it: whoever gets in sees all of your holdings.

If you notice misuse, write to contato@bipview.com and change your password.

4. What you connect to BipView

You choose what to connect, and there are two paths — both read-only:

  • Exchange API key, read-only. Create the key with no withdrawal permission and no trading permission. We check this and warn you on screen if the key can withdraw. The key is stored encrypted.
  • Public wallet address. It is the address you share to receive — it is already visible on the blockchain to anyone.

Never asked, never paste it: BipView never asks for your seed phrase or private key, on any screen or through any channel. If someone asks for it in our name, it is a scam.

By connecting a source, you declare that it is yours or that you are authorised to access it.

5. The figures you see are estimates

The values shown come from public sources and from the exchanges themselves, and are converted using market quotes. They are there for you to follow along, not to reconcile down to the cent.

The price of an old transaction is our own estimate, computed at the minute the operation took place. When we have no quote for an asset or a date, the screen says so — we do not invent a number in its place.

The official statement is your exchange's, and the truth of a transaction is what is on the blockchain. A discrepancy? The source wins, not us.

6. Tax: a mirror, not a return

BipView computes an estimate of your tax position to help you understand where you stand. It does not replace your accountant, it is not tax advice, and it is not your tax return.

The official return can be filed with Declare Cripto, our partner — and BipView receives a referral commission. We say it here because you have the right to know.

Responsibility for what is filed with the tax authority is yours.

7. Plans, billing and cancellation

BipView has a free plan and paid plans. What each one includes is described in the app, on the plans screen, before any charge.

When there is a subscription, it is billed through your device's store (Google Play or the App Store), under that store's billing and refund rules. Cancelling is done in the store itself, and it takes effect from the next cycle — the period already paid stays available until it ends.

If the price changes, we tell you before the change applies to you.

8. Availability and third-party sources

We work to keep the service up, but we do not promise uninterrupted operation: there is maintenance, network failure and, above all, dependence on third parties — exchanges, blockchain explorers and price sources.

When a source is down, the screen says so instead of faking a number. A reading that did not arrive is not a zero balance.
